Retail gallery

The retail gallery is located in the common ground floor of the district, connecting all six office buildings. Clear navigation, 4.5-meter displays, sound and convenient logistics — all that will provide 10,000 guests and residents of the business district with easy access. They will be able to use structured parking lots with 128 spaces for cars coming from the Moscow Ring Road. The 8,500 sq. m area has restaurants and cafes with summer patios offering views on the lake, and a selection of shops, beauty parlors, laundries and other service facilities.
